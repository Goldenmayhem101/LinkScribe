Every weapon now has real art, its own swing, a slash trail and its own hit effect. Tap Common, Glowing or Legendary to see how each weapon changes as you upgrade it.

The game no longer loops. Every world is a real mysterious place with its own look, hazards and stage names, and its monsters suit the place (fire monsters in volcanoes, sea creatures in the ocean). Every stage brings new monsters, stage 3 has an elite (⭐, glows gold) and stage 5 a boss. Some stages also have a surprise elite. Tap any stage to watch it.

The Monster Book as players will see it. Monsters you have met are shown. The rest stay dark, but you can see their name and which world they live in, so there is always something to look forward to.

For you only, not in the game: every monster moving and attacking. 155 now use your new 2D game art (from ChatGPT); the rest still use the older restyled art until their new drawings arrive.

Every world has its own boss, chosen from your monsters: bigger, with a red aura and a health bar, and its own attack. Here are the first 12.

5 chests, from Wooden to Crystal. Tap a chest to open it: it shakes, opens, the loot flies out, then a window shows exactly what you got before you collect it.

A world map instead of a long list: your path through every world (5 stages each, boss on the 5th), your stars on every stage, a crown on boss stages, and worlds unlock when you beat the boss before them.

Up to 3 stars per stage: finish it, collect every coin, take no damage. Stars unlock the Crystal chest, which gives players a reason to replay.
