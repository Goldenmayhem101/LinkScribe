Full description (4000 max):
Roll the dice, race your four pawns home and bump your rivals back to start! Ludora is the classic ludo board game with stunning boards, collectable heroes and fair play built in.

FAIR DICE FOR EVERYONE
• Every roll is pure chance: a 1 in 6 for every number, for you and for the computer
• No boosts, no lucky rolls, no paid dice. The best player wins, not the biggest wallet
• Harder computer levels only make smarter moves, they never get better dice
• No pop-up ads. Videos only play when you choose to watch one for a reward

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Vs Computer: four levels, from Easy to Expert
• Team 2 vs 2: you and a partner against two rivals
• Pass & Play: 2 to 4 players on one phone
• Plays fully offline, anywhere

WEEKLY EVENTS AND PRIZES
• Activity Center with a new Weekly Event and a daily Mini Event
• Win event prizes to keep: Epic dice, Epic pawns, boards and frames
• Daily rewards, a free season pass and treasure chests to open

COLLECT AND SHOW OFF
• 44 boards: Molten Lava, Deep Ocean, Dragon Realm, Sakura Garden, Frost and more
• 102 pawn heroes, from cute to Legendary. Legendary heroes have their own finishing moves
• 94 dice to spin in 3D. Legendary dice come with their own guardian
• 99 picture frames, with glowing Legendary frames
• Unlock items with coins, by watching videos, or by winning events
• Animated emojis and gifts that fly across the board to other players

FOR EVERYONE, EVERYWHERE
• Available in 108 languages; Ludora picks your phone's language automatically
• Your first game teaches you as you play
• Big "YOUR TURN" marker, a countdown around your picture, and auto play if you step away
• New items and events arrive regularly, no app update needed

Ludora is a ludo dice game for families, friends and board game fans of all ages. 2 to 4 players, quick games, easy rules.
