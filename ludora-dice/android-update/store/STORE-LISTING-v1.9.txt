Roll the dice, race your four pawns home and bump your rivals back to start! Ludora is the classic ludo board game, rebuilt with stunning boards, collectable heroes, weekly events and fair play at its heart.

★ FAIR DICE FOR EVERYONE
• Every roll is pure chance: a 1 in 6 for every number, for you and for the computer
• No boosts, no lucky rolls, no paid dice. The best player wins
• Harder computer levels make smarter moves, never better dice
• No pop-up ads. Videos only play when you choose one for a reward, never during a match

HOW TO PLAY
Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. Move by the number you roll, and roll again on a 6. Land on a rival to send it back to start, but pawns on a star are safe. Bring all four pawns home first to win!

Ludora is a ludo dice game for families, friends and board game fans of all ages. 2 to 4 players, quick games, easy rules.
