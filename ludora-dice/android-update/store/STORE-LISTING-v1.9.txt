Full description (4000 max):
Roll the dice, race your four pawns home and bump your rivals back to start! Ludora is the classic ludo board game you grew up with, rebuilt with stunning boards, collectable heroes, weekly events and fair play at its heart. Quick to learn, hard to put down, and perfect for a five-minute break or a long game night with family and friends.

🎲 FAIR DICE FOR EVERYONE
• Every roll is pure chance: a 1 in 6 for every number, for you and for the computer
• No boosts, no lucky rolls and no paid dice. The best player wins, not the biggest wallet
• Harder computer levels only make smarter moves. They never get better dice
• Everything that affects a game is the same for every player. Collectables only change how things look
• No pop-up ads, ever. Videos only play when you choose to watch one for a reward, and never during a match

🏁 PLAY YOUR WAY

• Plays fully offline, so you can roll on a plane, on the bus or anywhere without Wi-Fi
• Daily Twist: a new rule every day to keep games fresh. Blitz with fewer pawns, Safe Haven with extra safe stars, Hunter where only start squares are safe, and more

HOW TO PLAY
Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your yard. Move around the board by the number you roll, and roll again every time you get a 6. Land on a rival's pawn to send it back to start, but watch out: pawns resting on a star are safe. Be the first to bring all four pawns home to win!

Ludora is a ludo dice game for families, friends and board game fans of all ages. 2 to 4 players, quick games, easy rules, endless fun.
