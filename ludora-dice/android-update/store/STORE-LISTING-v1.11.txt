Roll the dice, race your four pawns home and bump your rivals back to start! Ludo Mayhem is the classic ludo board game, rebuilt with stunning boards, collectable heroes, weekly events and fair play at its heart.

★ FAIR DICE FOR EVERYONE
• Every roll is pure chance: a 1 in 6 for every number, for you and for the computer
• No boosts, no lucky rolls, no paid dice. The best player wins
• Harder computer levels make smarter moves, never better dice
• No pop-up ads. Videos only play when you choose one for a reward, never during a match

★ PLAY YOUR WAY
• Vs Computer: four levels, from Easy to Expert
• Team 2 vs 2: team up with a partner against two rivals
• Pass & Play: 2 to 4 players on one phone
• Plays fully offline, anywhere
• Daily Twist: a new rule every day, like Blitz, Safe Haven and Hunter

★ WEEKLY EVENTS AND PRIZES
• A new Weekly Event every week and a daily Mini Event
• Win prizes you keep forever: Epic dice, Epic pawns, boards and frames
• Weekly Token Hunt, a free monthly Season Pass and daily rewards
• Treasure chests to win: Iron, Silver, Gold and the rare Crystal chest

★ COLLECT AND SHOW OFF
• 44 boards: Molten Lava, Deep Ocean, Dragon Realm, Sakura Garden, Cyber City and more
• 102 pawn heroes. Every Legendary hero has its own finishing move
• 94 dice to spin in 3D. Every Legendary dice has its own guardian
• 99 picture frames, including glowing Legendary frames
• Unlock items with coins, optional videos or event wins
• Animated emojis and gifts that fly across the board

★ FOR EVERYONE, EVERYWHERE
• Available in 108 languages, picked automatically from your phone
• Your progress saves to your Google Play account
• Your first game teaches you as you play
• Earn XP, level up and collect 12 achievement medals
• New boards, dice, heroes and events arrive regularly

HOW TO PLAY
Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. Move by the number you roll, and roll again on a 6. Land on a rival to send it back to start, but pawns on a star are safe. Bring all four pawns home first to win!

Ludo Mayhem is a ludo dice game for families, friends and board game fans of all ages. 2 to 4 players, quick games, easy rules.
