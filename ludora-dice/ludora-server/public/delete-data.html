Delete your Ludo Mayhem data

Ludo Mayhem (formerly called Ludora) · a game by Goldenmayhem · Android package com.goldenmayhem.ludoradice

Fastest way: in the game

  1. Open Ludo Mayhem and tap the ⚙ Settings button.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my online data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your online account is removed from our server straight away.

By email

If you can't open the game, email goldenmayhem.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your friend code (shown in the game under Friends) if you have it. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your online player name (nickname)
  • Your online player ID and friend code
  • Your friends list and friend requests (you are also removed from other players' lists)

Emoji, gift and quick-chat messages are passed between players during a game and are never stored, so there is nothing to delete.

What is kept

  • Your coins, items, level and settings stay on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes them from your phone.
  • We keep no copies or backups of deleted online accounts.

Google Play cloud save

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your saved progress is stored by Google in your own Google account, not on our server. To delete it, open the Google Play Games app or your Google Play Games settings, find Ludo Mayhem and delete its saved game data. You can also manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).
